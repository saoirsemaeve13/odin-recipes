Baked Oatmeal

Description

The best solution to a hectic morning is to have breakfast ready to go. This baked oatmeal is perfect to warm up and eat while rushing out the door, or savored topped with yogurt and berries.

Ingredients:

  • 3c rolled oats
  • 1c brown sugar
  • 2tsp cinnamon
  • 2tsp baking powder
  • 1tsp salt
  • 2 large eggs
  • 1c milk
  • 1/2c melted butter
  • 2tsp vanilla extract
  • 3/4c dried cranberries

Steps:

  1. Preheat the oven to 350 degrees. Grease a 9x13 pan.
  2. Mix together the oats, brown sugar, cinnamon, baking powder, and salt in a large bowl.
  3. Beat in the eggs, milk, butter, and vanilla. Gently stir in the dried cranberries.
  4. Spread the mixture into the prepared baking dish. Smooth the top so it's even.
  5. Bake in the preheated oven until the top is golden brown, about 40 minutes.
  6. Let cool 10 minutes before cutting and serving. This recipe stores well in the fridge for meal prep.

This recipe can be altered any number of ways. The fruit can be swapped out for any dried fruit or fresh berry. You can add in up to 1/2c of nuts or chocolate chips. You can swap the vanilla extract for maple extract. The options are truly limitless.
